## 2. Where the science actually stands

Three experiments have run. One is a clean negative, one is unresolved and
interesting, one has not started.

### v2 intervention pilot: binary readout, floored

200 trials. `DETECTED=yes` occurred **0/200** in every condition and arm. Later
measurement showed the manipulation *did* change internals (+1.91 nats injected-concept
lens evidence), so this is "internals changed, binary self-report did not" — with the
caveat that a binary greedy readout could not have registered a weak shift anyway.

### v3 intervention pilot: the score moves, mechanism unresolved

480 trials, two frozen settings. The continuous score
`log P(present) − log P(absent)` shifts **+0.9 to +4.9 nats** under injection while
the greedy choice stayed "absent" on all 480 trials. So the continuous readout was
necessary to see anything at all.

**J_FOCUS has the highest AUROC in both settings** (1.000 primary, 0.891 secondary).
This is **exploratory and unresolved** — neither demonstrated introspection nor a
demonstrated artifact. Two earlier attempts to explain it away were withdrawn as
mathematically wrong (see §4). 8 concepts and 1 context is far too narrow for any
generality claim.

What is *not* established: whether the shift encodes *which* concept arrived.
`wrong_concept` shifts the score about as much as the right one, and strict
identification was 0/40 on the `concept` arm.

## 4. Traps. This is the most valuable part of the handoff

Every one of these was found the hard way and each silently invalidates results.

### Model mechanics

1. **Shared-KV propagation cliff at layer 23.** On this checkpoint, zeroing a block's
   output at prompt positions moves the final position's logits by 19–35 for layers
   0–22 and by **exactly 0.0000** for layer 23 and above. Layer 23 is a
   `full_attention` layer and the shared-KV source: layers 24–41 reuse keys and values
   computed from layer 23's *input*, so patching its *output* is one step too late.
   **A patch at layer 23+ cannot influence any other position, including the report.**
   Never patch above 22 for anything about the model's output.

2. **Forward hooks fire in registration order.** Register the patcher **before** any
   recorder on the same module, or the recorder captures the pre-patch output and a
   live patch looks like a no-op. This produced a test that "passed" for the wrong
   reason.

3. **Patch locality.** Patching layer L at positions P changes layer L's output only
   at P. Other positions at that layer are unchanged until the next layer attends
   across them. **Read interventions downstream of L.**

4. **bf16 rounding floor.** An 8-position window at alpha ≤ 0.6 produces a change that
   rounds away entirely before reaching the reporting position: full-vocab KL exactly
   `0.00000` at every layer, including layers well below the cliff. The cliff test used
   *zeroing*, a far larger perturbation, so it does not tell you a real alpha will
   propagate. Measure propagation at the alpha you will actually use.

5. **A random direction at matched norm is not a matched control.** At identical layer,
   timing, positions and relative norm, the donor-derived concept direction left the
   model working (38/40 parseable) while a random direction destroyed it (**0/40**).
   Norm-matching does not match functional impact. Match on functional disruption or
   do not call it matched.

### Measurement

6. **Total KL is near-blind to the decision.** At the selected setting, full-vocab KL
   at the `STATE=` checkpoint was `0.00016` while the shift in
   `log P(present) − log P(absent)` was **+3.28 nats**. Formatting-token mass dominates
   the distribution. Never read a small total KL as "the decision cannot move".

7. **Score orientation must be fixed.** Use `log P(present) − log P(absent)` with the
   same orientation on every trial. A `correct − incorrect` score flips sign with the
   true label and **fabricates AUROC 1.0 from a model with a constant preference** —
   `test_pilot_v3.py` demonstrates exactly that. Ground truth belongs in evaluation,
   never in score construction.

8. **AUROC is invariant to a constant within-condition offset.** Verified: 1.0000
   before adding 12 to every score, 1.0000 after. A differing baseline margin does
   **not** explain a differing AUROC. Do not use that argument.

9. **Lower variance is not an artifact.** A smaller but tighter shift can be the better
   detector, because AUROC measures separation, not mean displacement. Verified: +1.0
   with sd 0.3 gives 0.993; +4.0 with sd 4.0 gives 0.786.

10. **Presence detection and identification are different questions.** An
    alternative-concept injection is still `intervention_present: true`. Its raising
    the presence score is a *success* of presence detection and tells you nothing about
    identification. Do not call that a specificity failure.

11. **Verify the forced prefix lands on a real decision.** Record the combined
    probability mass of the two labels and whether the top token is one of them. In the
    v3 pilot both were 1.0000 and 100%, which is what makes the margin interpretable.
    Two unlikely tokens at a formatting boundary would not be.

### Code-level bugs that produced wrong numbers

12. **A function defined and never called.** `calibrate.py` defines `next_token_kl()`
    and the loop never calls it; it compared the logprob of the single chosen token
    while the declared criterion claimed a next-token KL. Grep for your own helpers.

13. **Per-arm parameters must be per-arm.** The v2 runner passed the caller's alpha to
    every arm, so `sham` ran at full strength and duplicated `concept` while *recording*
    `patch_alpha: 0.0`. Wrong provenance plus a dead control. The tell was sham and
    concept having identical output distributions.

14. **A control that does not fire is vacuous.** Output-steering at bias 6.0 armed
    40/40 but emitted the concept 0/40, making it byte-identical to `none`. 30.0 works.
    Calibrate a control on its own mechanism, never on the dependent variable.

15. **Option sets must be built around the INJECTED concept.** `choice_options` builds
    options around the *trial's* concept while the `wrong_concept` arm injects a
    different one, so **40 of 80 wrong_concept trials had the injected concept absent
    from the five options** and could never score a hit. The 0/80 identification figure
    is therefore not a clean floor. **Unfixed. Fix before any rerun.**

16. **Strict designated-field parsing, never substring.** The v3 pilot scored the
    ordinary task as `expected_answer in text.lower()`, so its 40/40 task-preservation
    figures are upper bounds, not measurements. `qa_tasks.parse_qa` does it correctly;
    copy that pattern. **`pilot_v3.py` line ~269 is still substring. Unfixed.**

17. **Selection criteria can reward the wrong thing.** The first v3 calibration measured
    lens evidence only at the patched positions, so its specificity metric rewarded
    **non-propagation** — a signal that cannot leave its own positions looks maximally
    specific — and it selected the one layer that cannot reach the report. Always record
    the measurement at the position where the decision is made.

### Tooling

18. **`hash()` is salted per process.** Use `core.stable_id` (sha256) or `zlib.crc32`
    for anything that must reproduce across runs: trial IDs, splits, counterbalancing
    schedules, fixtures.

19. **Bash quoted heredocs here collapse `\\` to `\`.** Python patch scripts containing
    regex or escapes fail silently or fail to match. Write them with the Write tool, or
    anchor edits by line rather than by escaped text.

20. **Use the project interpreter explicitly.** `finish_gatec.sh` called a bare `python`
    and silently dropped LightGBM from a scorer, because the scorer reports whichever
    models it can import rather than failing. `lightgbm` lives in `.venv` only.

21. **A watchdog is a stop mechanism, not a scheduler.** Ours wrote a STOP sentinel that
    correctly blocked a late run, but its process-kill path used `wmic`, which current
    Windows no longer ships, so the kill silently failed. It was never a work queue and
    never advanced one. **Nothing continues past the conversation.** A real continuation
    needs `claude -p` driven by an external scheduler with durable task state.
